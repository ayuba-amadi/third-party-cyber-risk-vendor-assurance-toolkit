# Final limitations

- Fictional data only. Evidence consists of status, dates and references to fictional documents.
- Session changes expire on reset or session loss. Export records to retain demonstration work. Shared persistence and concurrent approval controls require a separate operating environment.
- The app records claimed reviewer and approver roles. It offers no authentication or production authorisation.
- A selected report date drives flags and date-sensitive logic. A dated report cannot replay every past evidence review or reconstruct a past workspace.
- Earlier versions depend on retained IDs, snapshots and source history. CSV history is not an independently protected audit log.
- Policy weights, bands, overrides and exception handling belong to the capstone methodology. Independent sign-off is pending.
- The browser compares persisted reassessment versions. New version and change-event entry requires a controlled CSV copy and semantic validation. A browser-only reassessment authoring flow is outside the implemented scope.
- Hosting, live health and rollback are outside the local verification cycle.
