# Demonstration script

Set **Report date** to 18 November 2026. Use the baseline fixtures. Check the test and defect registers for observed results and gaps before presenting.

1. Open **Vendor Register**. Add VEN-011, Live Demo Vendor. Use a fictional owner role and future review date. Use Internal data, None access, dependency 2 and Moderate replaceability. Validate it and save in the session.
2. Calculate criticality. Point out the four factors, score of 5, Medium band and absent override. Check that no decision was created.
3. Edit the saved vendor. Select Privileged access and dependency 4. Compare against the earlier values. Explain the expected increase of four points to 9 and High. Show any reassessment warning. For an already assessed vendor, a factor change triggers a reassessment warning while preserving the saved assessment factors.
4. Select VEN-002 and ASMT-002 in **Assessment** and **Evidence Review**. Show missing AM-01 and DP-01, adverse treatment and Incomplete status.
5. Select VEN-009. Show the DP-01 confirmed failure and High rating with the unrelated verified scores. Open **Reports** to review findings, actions and separate decision DEC-008.
6. Open **Decisions** for VEN-003 and display DEC-002 and ACT-001. Explain the conditions, responsible party, deadline and review date. For a live entry use a separate current assessment awaiting a decision with a linked fictional action. Leave conditions blank in the preview first, then enter conditions and valid dates. Confirm only after explicit review.
7. Open **Remediation** and show ACT-001 overdue by 18 days.
8. Open **Decisions**, select VEN-006 and show DEC-005 as expired. Check VEN-009 remains rejected. Show the report-date control.
9. Open **Reassessment**, select VEN-004 and compare ASMT-004 with ASMT-011. Show CHG-001 and CHG-002, historical decisions and the current assessment awaiting a decision.
10. Reset the session before the baseline comparison. Open **Reports**. Reconcile total vendors, categories, bands, decisions, incomplete assessments, overdue actions and concentration with the expected fixture and QA report.
